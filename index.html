The problem

Four things go wrong for a school every day

The story we demo: Sunil and Kumari live near Hatton. Their daughter Dinithi has won a Grade 6 place at a school in Kandy. She will travel alone. They want to know she arrived.

Parents

Can't see the school day

A child leaves at dawn. The family finds out how the day went when she gets home.

Admissions

Everyone

The arrival escalation ladder

The strongest moment of the demo. Each step only fires if the one before got no answer.

  • 7:30 No check-in. WhatsApp to the parent: 1 absent, 2 late, 3 call me.
  • 7:45 No reply. AI voice call (Safe Arrival Caller).
  • 8:00 Still unclear. High-priority request to the Duty Teachers ring group.
  • Any time Gate QR scan sends "arrived" within seconds and turns the board green.

How we take it to market

Pilot with one schoolA Kandy school like the one in our story. Run one admissions season and one term of arrival alerts.
Publish the proofUse the pilot numbers and a short video as the case study for the next school.
Ride the ad seasonPitch before admissions open. Facebook ads send parents straight into WhatsApp and the CRM.
Grow by referralPrincipals talk to principals. Offer school groups one dashboard across branches.

Pricing idea to validate: a monthly subscription based on student count, with WhatsApp, SMS and call costs passed through at cost, and a one-time setup fee for importing classes, guardians and students. No prices are set yet.
