01 The problem

Four things go wrong for a school every day

Schools run on phone calls, paper notes and staff goodwill. Parents are left guessing, and the office answers the same questions all day.

Parents

Can't see the school day

A child leaves at dawn. The family finds out how the day went when she gets home in the evening.

Admissions

Enquiries die on missed calls

Questions arrive at night and on weekends. Nobody answers, and the family tries another school.

Office

The same questions, daily

Fees, exam dates and documents, asked by phone in three languages, all day long.

Everyone

News arrives late

Fee dues, marks and teacher concerns reach parents weeks late, on paper, or not at all.

The family in our story

Dinithi has passed the Grade 5 scholarship exam and won a Grade 6 place at a school in Kandy, one to two hours from home near Hatton. She will travel alone by public bus. Her parents want one thing: to know she arrived.

05 Solution blueprint

Everything a school needs, configured on Uniconnect: the data model, who can see what, every channel, the AI agents, 24 workflows, campaigns, integrations and dashboards.

Everyone sees exactly what their job needs

Permissions are set per role and per module at three levels: All records, Group (records assigned to the user's group) and Own (records assigned to the user). Sensitive fields are locked to named roles, and dashboards are shared view-only by role.

Language rule

Each voice agent speaks one language. English and Tamil voice agents come first; Sinhala voice is used only if it passes our quality test. Otherwise Sinhala-speaking parents get the recorded IVR plus Sinhala WhatsApp text, which works well.

How the agents are instructed

AISchoolLine AssistantWhatsApp and web chat 
You are SchoolLine, the parent assistant for [School]. Reply in the parent's language (Sinhala, Tamil or English), in short WhatsApp-style messages.
If the parent is a known guardian, discuss only their own children, using their records: today's attendance, fees, results, meetings.
If they are a new enquiry, help with admissions: explain fees, documents and the visit, and ask for the child's name, grade, current school and home town.
Answer school facts only from approved school information. If unsure, say so and offer to pass the question to the office.
Never give medical advice. If a parent sounds worried about a child's safety, tell them the duty teacher is being informed and hand off immediately.
The school does not provide transport; do not promise transport arrangements.
AISafe Arrival CallerVoice campaign at 7:45 
You are calling from [School] because [child] has not checked in today.
Greet the parent by name, say you are an automated assistant from the school, and ask if [child] is absent today or on the way.
Capture: absent / running late / don't know, the reason, and the expected arrival time.
If the parent does not know where the child is, or is worried, say you are connecting them to the duty teacher now and transfer.
Keep the call under one minute. Stay calm and reassuring.
