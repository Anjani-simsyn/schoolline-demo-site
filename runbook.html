Short on time

The 6-minute version

Show one document instead of four. Skip the whisper beat and the judge-phone escalation. Show the through-term scene as one slide.

If something fails

Never debug on stage

Play the backup recording of that scene and move on. Every live scene is screen-recorded at the dress rehearsal.

WhatsApp window

Text only reaches recent chats

Free-text replies only reach numbers that messaged the business number in the last 24 hours. Have Phone A and the judge phone message it before the show.
